Until today, Shipcast was a command: `shipcast launch` prepared the drafts for a launch and I reviewed them in a GitHub pull request.

Now it has a web app. From it I see every launch and which channels it was published on, connect the accounts for each network (with OAuth on Mastodon and Instagram, and the secrets in the system keychain), review and edit the drafts before approving them, and launch a repo by choosing its release.

I can also write an article of my own, like this one. Shipcast translates it, prepares a piece for each channel I choose and publishes it on the blog, with review or without it.

This article is the proof: I wrote it in the app and published it without going through a pull request.
